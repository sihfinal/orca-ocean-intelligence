## 📌 1. MAIN PAGE / INTRODUCTION

> *"Hello everyone! We are **Team `SIH_Hackers`**, and we built **Blue Orbit** directly for **ISRO Problem Statement 26176: ORCA (Marine EcOsystem Reasoning with Collaborative Agents)** under the theme of **Disaster Management**.*
>
> *Every single day, massive volumes of **satellite Earth Observation (EO)** and oceanographic data—including **Sea Surface Temperature (SST)**, **chlorophyll concentration**, and meteorological forecasts—are generated by **ISRO Oceansat-3 (OCM-3)**, **INSAT-3DR**, and **INCOIS**. But for over **4 million coastal fishermen**, researchers, and maritime operators, interpreting raw raster layers and heterogeneous public domain datasets is nearly impossible.*
>
> *That is why we developed **Blue Orbit**: an **Agentic AI-powered conversational marine decision-support platform**. By integrating **conversational intelligence** with **geospatial technologies**, our system autonomously interprets user intent, performs **spatial-temporal reasoning**, coordinates **collaborative autonomous AI agents**, and delivers synthesized, **explainable, evidence-based recommendations** directly in **13 Indian regional languages** with **voice and offline caching**."*

## 💬 2. CHATBOT PAGE (`AI Chatbot Studio`)

> *"Now, coming to the **AI Chatbot Studio**—this is the **conversational intelligence** core of our platform, built with a minimalist interface called **'Reasoning by Design'** to support natural, **contextual, multi-turn conversations**.*
>
> *Right in the center, we feature one-tap scenario buttons addressing the exact operational queries specified in **ISRO’s problem statement**:*
> * **🐟 Tuna PFZ Advisory** — Pinpoints high-yield **Potential Fishing Zones (PFZs)** based on **favourable sea surface temperature** and **chlorophyll concentration gradients**.
> * **🛡️ Sea Safety Clearance** — Evaluates hydrodynamic conditions to answer: *'Is it safe to venture into the sea tomorrow morning?'* with an objective **0 to 100 Safety Index score**.
> * **📥 Cache Audio Packs** — Preloads regional voice models into local device memory for seamless **offline vernacular interaction** at deep sea.
>
> *At the bottom is our multimodal input capsule with **automatic language identification** and **speech-to-text voice recognition**. A fisherman can simply speak or type in their native tongue. The system autonomously decomposes their request into executable tasks and routes it through our multi-agent pipeline."*

---

## 🗺️ 3. GIS COMMAND PAGE (`GIS Command Center`)

> *"Here is how a user interacts with the **GIS Command Center**, delivering the **interactive geospatial visualizations** and **GIS layer correlation** demanded by ISRO:*
>
> * **Tapping a PFZ Hotspot**: When the user clicks any **green fish marker**, the system reveals the **Potential Fishing Zone** telemetry—target pelagic species like Tuna, recommended net depth, SST front, and catch enhancement multiplier.
> * **A\* Least-Cost Safe Navigation Route**: Selecting that hotspot instantly computes this blue path—an **A\* least-cost, obstacle-aware navigation route** that optimizes vessel transit time and fuel burn while steering clear of rough seas and high-wave zones.
> * **Enforcing Geofencing Restrictions**: On the left **Geospatial Layers panel**, the user can toggle safety boundaries. The dashed line visualizes the **UNCLOS 1982 International Maritime Boundary Line (IMBL)** and **200 Nautical Mile Indian Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ)**, providing proactive geofencing alerts before approaching restricted waters, foreign boundaries, or **Marine Protected Areas (MPAs) & ecologically sensitive reserves**.
> * **Spatial-Temporal Point Investigation**: Clicking any random oceanic coordinate fetches live **tide, weather, and sea state observations**—displaying **significant wave height**, swell period, **wind speed**, Beaufort scale, and safety ratings on the live bottom telemetry bar.
> * **Forecast Exploration**: The top **Temporal Control** lets users switch between real-time observation and forecast windows to explore upcoming sea conditions."*

---

## 🧠 4. AGENT DAG PAGE (`Agent DAG Studio`)

> *"Now, coming to the **Agent DAG Studio**—this is the actual engine room demonstrating **Agentic AI, autonomous planning, tool selection, task execution**, and **collaborative multi-agent reasoning**:*
>
> * **Running a Query**: The user types a query or selects preset prompts like *'Nearest Tuna PFZ (Kochi)'* or *'Cyclone Warnings (Bay of Bengal)'* and clicks **Run DAG**.
> * **Collaborative DAG Execution**: The platform coordinates **6 specialized domain agents** in a **Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG)**:
>   1. **Master Supervisor DAG Planner** — Decomposes complex natural language intent into an execution plan.
>   2. **Marine EO Data Agent** — Discovers and ingests ISRO Oceansat-3 and INSAT-3DR telemetry.
>   3. **Weather & Marine Hazard Agent** — Evaluates squalls, high waves, lightning risks, and **cyclone alert cones** for **disaster management**.
>   4. **Ocean Analytics & PFZ Engine** — Performs thermal-chlorophyll front coincidence and habitat suitability analysis.
>   5. **Geospatial & Geofencing Agent** — Enforces IMBL 12 NM boundary compliance and solves safe navigational routing.
>   6. **Neural Synthesis & Multilingual Agent** — Synthesizes natural language recommendations grounded in verifiable evidence.
> * **Explainable AI (XAI) & Step-by-Step Trace**: Clicking **'Show 6 Agent Step Traces'** reveals the transparent provenance chain—displaying each agent's individual thought reasoning, execution latency in milliseconds, and mathematical output.
> * **Voice Playback**: Users can tap the **Listen button** for instant speech playback in their regional language."*

---

## ⚓ 5. SAFETY BAROMETER PAGE (`Sea Safety & Disaster Barometer`)

> *"Coming to the **Safety Barometer**—our dedicated **Disaster Management and Risk Assessment hub**, specifically designed to protect fishermen safety:*
>
> * **Harbour Directory**: Users can filter across all coastal states and select their home port—such as **Kochi, Kasimedu Chennai, Sassoon Dock Mumbai, Visakhapatnam, or Porbandar**.
> * **Proactive Status Indicators**: Every harbour features a real-time status beacon—**Green for Safe, Amber for Caution, and Red for Hazardous No-Venture**.
> * **Sea Venture Clearance Verdict**: Selecting a port immediately outputs the official verdict—**SAFE FOR VENTURE**, **EXERCISE CAUTION**, or **HAZARDOUS NO VENTURE**—coupled with a calibrated **0 to 100 Sea Safety Score**.
> * **Actionable Marine Directives**: Plain-language operational advice instructs motorized boats and mechanized deep-sea trawlers on safe offshore limits.
> * **Meteorological Telemetry Meters**: Displays 4 core real-time parameters: **significant wave height & swell period**, **wind speed in knots & Beaufort scale**, **sea visibility**, and **cyclone / lightning strike probabilities**."*

## 📋 6. ADVISORY BULLETIN PAGE (`Official Marine Advisory Bulletin`)

> *"The **Advisory Bulletin** serves as our official reporting dashboard, bridging ISRO, INCOIS, and coastal maritime authorities:*
>
> * **Executive Synthesis Cards**: Instantly summarizes the official sea venture verdict, 24-hour validity period, detected ocean front count, and sea state.
> * **High-Resolution PFZ Recommendation Table**: Catalogs validated fishing grounds with precise **WGS-84 coordinates**, target species, gear recommendations, and confidence ratings.
> * **One-Click Spatial Sync**: Clicking **'View on Map ➔'** instantly switches to the GIS Command map, locking onto the coordinates and generating the safe transit route.
> * **Official PDF Export with QR Verification**: Clicking **'Print / Export Official PDF'** generates a certified marine bulletin bearing a **tamper-proof cryptographic QR verification token**, ready for inspection by port officers and the Indian Coast Guard."*
